Conference Themes

1. AI for Mathematical Reasoning

  • Leading models and the global AI landscape.
  • Mathematical reasoning agents and harnesses.
  • Training, evaluation, limitations, and challenges for mathematicians and AI developers.

2. Formalizing Mathematics with Lean

  • AI-assisted formalization and theorem proving.
  • Combining Lean with LLMs.
  • Future integration of Lean into mathematical research.

3. Mathematics in the Age of AI

  • New individual and collective research practices.
  • Mathematical discoveries and opportunities in physics.
  • The evolving role of mathematicians.
