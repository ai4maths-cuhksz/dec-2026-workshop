PARTICIPATION DETAILS

Frequently asked questions

Is the date confirmed?+

3 December 2026 is the proposed date. Please wait for the final announcement before making non-refundable travel arrangements.

Can students participate?+

The meeting is being planned for students and researchers interested in AI for mathematics. Check the registration form for current capacity and participation arrangements.

Will there be remote talks or online attendance?+

Online participation and any streaming arrangements are still being considered. Confirmed information will appear here.

Can I suggest a mathematical problem?+

Yes. The existing open-problem database accepts contributions independently of this event. Visit the open-problems page for the link and a preparation template. The preliminary conference program does not include a dedicated problem-pitch session.

Who should I contact?+

The December organizing committee and contact address will be announced here.

For questions about the meeting, email cosmelouart@cuhk.edu.cn.
